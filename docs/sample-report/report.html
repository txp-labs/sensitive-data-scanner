PCI DSS

Card verification codes or PINs were also found. PCI DSS does not allow keeping these after a payment is authorized, even encrypted (requirement 3.3.1).

Your Qualified Security Assessor (QSA) or acquiring bank decides whether PCI DSS applies, and to which systems.

US breach-notification laws

Social Security numbers, ITINs, account numbers or card numbers were found. US state breach-notification laws generally treat a Social Security number or ITIN kept with a person's name as personal information, and an account or card number too when it is kept with the code or password that opens the account. If they were exposed, you may have to notify the people affected and, in some states, regulators. If your business is a financial institution under the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act, which includes lenders and tax preparers, the FTC Safeguards Rule may also apply.

If the data stays: Left in place, each copy is one more place an exposure could start, and one more place to search and report on if one happens. Many of these laws ease the duty to notify when the exposed data was encrypted and the key was not exposed with it.

Your legal counsel decides which laws apply. It depends on where the people affected live, not only on where your business is.
