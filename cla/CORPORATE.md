# Corporate Contributor License Agreement

> **DRAFT: pending legal review.** The structure follows the Apache Software
> Foundation's Corporate CLA: a license grant, not a transfer of ownership.

**Version 0.1 (draft), 2026-09-29**

This agreement lets an organization ("the Corporation") authorize its employees
and contractors to contribute to `txp-labs/sensitive-data-scanner` and any other
project that adopts this agreement ("the Project"), maintained by
**txp-labs, LLC** ("txp-labs").

## 1. Scope

This agreement covers Contributions submitted by the individuals the Corporation
lists in Schedule A ("Designated Employees"), as updated from time to time by
written notice to txp-labs.

## 2. Licenses

For each Contribution by a Designated Employee, the Corporation grants the same
copyright license (including the right to distribute under other license terms)
and the same patent license as section 3 and section 4 of the
[Individual CLA](INDIVIDUAL.md), on the same terms. The Corporation keeps
ownership of its copyright.

## 3. What the Corporation promises

- It is legally entitled to grant these licenses.
- Each Designated Employee is authorized to submit Contributions on its behalf.
- It will notify txp-labs when someone is no longer authorized, and will
  identify any third-party work included in a Contribution.

## 4. No other obligations

The Corporation provides Contributions "AS IS" and is not expected to support
them.

## 5. How to sign

Send the signed agreement, with Schedule A, to the address given here once this
draft is final (to be confirmed during legal review), from an
authorized signatory. txp-labs will confirm, and add the listed GitHub accounts
to the CLA allowlist.
